About

I started out wanting to be a fashion designer. Somewhere between sketchbooks and Computer Science, I found product design, the place where art, empathy and logic meet.

I'm a B.Tech Computer Science student from Kanpur who designs and builds. Writing real code taught me how products work behind the screen, and designing them is what I enjoy most: understanding people, simplifying their journey, and making every screen feel considered.
